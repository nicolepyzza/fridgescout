# Privacy Policy for FridgeScout

**Last Updated: December 21, 2025**

## Data Collection
FridgeScout does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

## What Data is Stored
All data you enter (food items, expiration dates, barcodes) is stored locally on your device only using the app's secure local storage (AsyncStorage). This data:
- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to any servers
- Is not shared with third parties
- Is automatically deleted if you uninstall the app

## Permissions
FridgeScout requests the following permissions:
- **Camera Access**: Used only for barcode scanning. No photos or videos are saved or transmitted.
- **Notifications**: Used only to send you reminders about expiring food items. All notifications are generated locally on your device.

## No Accounts Required
FridgeScout does not require user accounts or login credentials.

## No Analytics or Tracking
We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or any third-party tracking services.

## Children's Privacy
FridgeScout does not knowingly collect any information from children.

## Changes to Privacy Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of any changes.
