## Description

**Never waste food again with FridgeScout!**

FridgeScout helps you track what's in your fridge and alerts you before food expires. Simply scan barcodes or manually add items, set expiration dates, and get timely reminders.

**KEY FEATURES:**
• Barcode Scanner - Quickly add items by scanning
• Manual Entry - Add items without barcodes
• Expiration Tracking - See at a glance what's expiring soon
• Smart Reminders - Get notifications before food goes bad
• Days Counter - Track how long items have been in your fridge
• Color-Coded Warnings - Visual alerts for expired and expiring items
• Simple & Clean Interface - Easy to use, no learning curve

**REDUCE FOOD WASTE**
Americans waste 30-40% of food. FridgeScout helps you use what you have before it spoils, saving money and reducing environmental impact.

**PRIVACY FIRST**
All data stays on your device. No account required, no data collection.

**PERFECT FOR:**
✓ Busy families
✓ Meal planners
✓ Anyone wanting to reduce food waste
✓ Budget-conscious shoppers
✓ Organized home cooks

Download FridgeScout today and start saving food and money!
